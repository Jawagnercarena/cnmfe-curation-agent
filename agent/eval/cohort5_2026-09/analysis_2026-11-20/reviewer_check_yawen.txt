   correlation of the high-passed Cn. A pair is a RELIABLE edge when at least two of the three agree
   within 3 px. Sessions linked by reliable edges form one field; every session gets a position by
   least squares over those edges, so every pair in the field gets a shift by composition, refined to
   the best vote count within +/-4 px ('votes'; 'next' = best count > 10 px away = chance level).

   matching candidate was kept, 'deleted' if matched only to reviewed deletes, 'auto' if matched only to
   auto-rejected candidates, 'none' if no candidate. Cells whose centre falls within 15 px of B's edge
   are left out. A cell can be silent on another day, so the within-reviewer rows are the baseline.
